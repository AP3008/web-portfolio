# web-portfolio

Adam's portfolio site, with a 3D desk experience and a 2D site that share one theme and one set of content.

## Language

### Fitness

**Live Pulse**:
The card in the Body Building modal that shows Adam's recovery, strain and sleep performance for one Whoop day, labelled with that day's date.
_Avoid_: today's stats, health widget

**Whoop day**:
One Whoop cycle, running from one sleep to the next (not a calendar day). It is dated by the day it started, in Adam's timezone.
_Avoid_: today, calendar day

**Public metric**:
A Whoop number the site may show visitors: recovery %, day strain, sleep performance %. Every other Whoop value (HRV, resting HR, body weight) is private and never leaves the server.
_Avoid_: stats, health data
